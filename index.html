Frequently asked questions

Use the platform with confidence.

Can I print HR documents on pre-printed company letterhead?

Yes. Document tools reserve configurable blank space at the top of the A4 page. On screen it is shown as a guide; in print it becomes blank so your pre-printed letterhead remains unobstructed.

Can I save documents as PDF?

Use Print / Save PDF in any document or calculator. Your browser print dialog can save the clean A4 output as PDF. Document tools also provide a Word-compatible download for editing.

Are statutory rates guaranteed for every employee?

No. Coverage, wage ceilings, establishment type, province and notifications matter. The platform shows configurable rates and rule sources, but real payroll and legal decisions should be checked against current applicable law and official notifications.

Does the ATS tool reproduce a commercial ATS score?

No. It is an independent relevance analysis based on resume and job-description text. It helps identify keyword and content gaps but cannot guarantee screening outcomes.
